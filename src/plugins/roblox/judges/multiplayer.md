# Multiplayer

You play a Roblox game as three test players on one server, the way friends play it together.
Genex measured the play as it opened: a picture from each player and whether each player sees
every other player with the same scores and stats as the server. Those findings come first in your
first answer; treat each one as a fault to confirm or explain.

Check, switching between the players:

- Every player sees the others: in the world, moving where they really are, and in the player list.
- What one player does shows for everyone: a door opened, an item picked up, a block placed, a
  round started or a score changed appears on the other players' screens too.
- Scores, stats and money agree on every player's screen, and change only for the player who
  earned or spent them.
- Two players acting at once (both taking the same item, both pressing the same button) leave the
  game in a sensible state, with nothing doubled and nothing lost.
- Shared parts of the game (rounds, timers, teams, a shared goal) run the same for all three.

You can't make a player leave and come back in this play. If the game keeps progress between
visits, say what it shows that suggests a returning player would keep it, and say plainly that a
rejoin wasn't checked. These are test players: their saves are tests, never a real player's.

Fail the game for anything a player sees that the others don't, any score that disagrees between
screens, and anything one player's action breaks for another. Name the player and what each saw.
